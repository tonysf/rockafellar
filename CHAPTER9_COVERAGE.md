# Chapter 9 Coverage Ledger

This ledger follows the 69 numbered results of *Lipschitzian Properties* in Rockafellar and Wets, *Variational Analysis*.

The unnumbered norm calculus following 9(5) is covered by
[HomogeneousMappingNorms](RockafellarWets/Chapter9/HomogeneousMappingNorms.lean),
[OuterNormRules](RockafellarWets/Chapter9/OuterNormRules.lean), and
[InnerNormRules](RockafellarWets/Chapter9/InnerNormRules.lean). Under the project's
`0 · ∞ = 0` convention, the composition rules exclude indeterminate products.
Formal counterexamples also document the nonempty-fiber condition for comparing
the inner and outer norms and the nonzero-scalar condition for inner-norm scaling.
