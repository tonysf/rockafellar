The unnumbered minimum formula before 7.48 requires a qualification. The lower epi-limit commutes with pointwise minimum. Alternating heights 0 and 1 give strict inequality for the upper epi-limit, even for finite functions. When the two input sequences epi-converge, their minimum epi-converges as stated. [EpiLatticeOperations](RockafellarWets/Chapter7/EpiLatticeOperations.lean).

The converse in the remark after 7.23 fails. The function that equals the absolute value on the rationals and its negative elsewhere has a proper epi-derivative at zero, as does its negative, but the semiderivative does not exist in every direction. [EpiDerivativeCounterexample](RockafellarWets/Chapter7/EpiDerivativeCounterexample.lean).
